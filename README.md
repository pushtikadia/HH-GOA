## 🌺 About the Project

**HH Goa 2026 — Builder ID Generator** is a lightweight, interactive web application that lets builders create a personalized digital Builder ID card directly in the browser.

Choose a profile photo, enter your **Name**, **Date**, and **Location**, adjust your photo positioning, preview the card in real time, and download the finished design as a high-resolution PNG.

The visual direction blends:

> **Goan coastal warmth × heritage architecture × tropical colors × modern hacker culture**

No backend. No database. No complicated setup.

Just open the page, personalize your card, and build your own Goa moment. 🌴
