## 🌴 Overview

**HH Goa 2026 — Builder ID Generator** is a lightweight, browser-based
application that allows builders to create a personalized digital identity
for Hacker House Goa.

The project combines a modern builder-focused experience with a visual
language inspired by **Goa's coastline, tropical greenery, warm architecture,
sunlight, beaches, and vibrant culture**.

Users can upload their photograph, customize their **name, date, and location**,
adjust their profile image, preview the Builder ID in real time, and download
the final result as a high-resolution image.

The entire experience runs on the client side and requires no backend,
database, authentication, or installation.

---

## 🌊 Why We Built It

For **HH Goa 2026 Task 1**, the goal was to create something simple, useful,
and visually connected to the spirit of Goa.

Instead of creating just another static event badge, we wanted builders to
have something they could actually personalize and use as part of their
digital identity.

The concept brings together:

> **Builder Identity × Goa × Personalization**

The result is a simple experience where every builder can create a card
that feels like their own.

---

## 🏝️ Design Philosophy

The visual direction is built around a balance between:

> **GOAN COASTAL CHARACTER × MODERN BUILDER CULTURE**

The design takes inspiration from:

- 🌊 Arabian Sea and Mandovi waters
- 🌴 Coconut palms and tropical greenery
- ☀️ Goan sunlight
- 🏖️ Sunlit beaches and warm sand
- 🏛️ Goan heritage architecture
- 🎨 Vibrant festival colors
- 💻 Modern technology and builder culture

Rather than recreating Goa as a traditional tourism poster, these elements
are translated into a **clean, modern digital identity system**.
